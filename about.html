About Luna's Wicks and Wonders

Welcome to Luna's Wicks & Wonders. What began as a personal passion for creating cozy, intentional home rituals blossomed into a dream to share that magic with your space while building a meaningful legacy for my children. Hand-poured under the glow of intention, our candles bridge the gap between crisp nature and soothing warmth.

Family and mindfulness are poured into every single jar we create. We carefully craft each small batch with the hope of lighting your wonder—one wick at a time—bringing the peace and beauty of the night sky straight into your home. Thank you for welcoming a piece of our universe into yours and supporting a business built genuinely from the heart.

All the candles are handmade with love and care with hopes to light your wonder one wick at a time.
