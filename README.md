# lunaswicks&wonders.github.io
This site is for Luna's Wicks & Wonders, a fictional candle store and home-based micro business. We sell a variety of scented/aromatherapy candles, as well as decorative candles. You can browse our selection and purchase candles directly through our online shop, or if you're interested in a custom-made candle that we don't normally sell or have in stock, you can fill out our request form. We welcome all candle lovers; whether you're looking for a gift for a loved one or a special treat for yourself, our website welcomes you. For any inquiries, I will respond to all messages within 24-48 hours.
